Texas Cottage Food Educator Toolkit Sample

Included in the full toolkit:
- Label review exercise with required Texas disclosure and allergen prompts
- Launch checklist worksheet for first-time sellers
- Market-day prep sheet for packaging, signage, and ingredient backup notes
- Facilitator talking points linked to current DSHS guidance

Sample classroom prompt:
1. Read the example cookie label.
2. Identify the operation name, product name, ingredient list, allergen statement, net quantity, and disclosure.
3. Decide whether the seller still needs a date-made field or safe-handling statement.
4. Discuss how the answer changes for refrigerated products or online orders.
